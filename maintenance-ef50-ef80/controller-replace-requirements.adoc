= Requirements to replace a controller - EF50 and EF80
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Before replacing the controller in your EF50 or EF80 storage system, ensure you meet the requirements for a successful replacement. This includes verifying you have a failed controller, verifying the state of your storage system, acquiring tools for the procedure, and having a management station and browser to access SANtricity System Manager.

.What's next?

After you’ve the met the requirements to replace the impaired controller, you need to link:controller-replace-prepare.html[prepare your storage system].
